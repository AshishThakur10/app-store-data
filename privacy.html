WaterMinder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Overview

WaterMinder ("the app") is a hydration tracker made by Ashish Thakur. This policy explains what information the app handles. The app is designed to be private by default: no ads, no analytics, and no third-party data collection.

Data handled by the app

On-device data (stored only on your device)

  • Daily water-intake entries (amounts and timestamps)
  • Your daily goal and reminder preferences
  • Local family profiles (names you create)
  • Leaderboard history

This data lives in a local database on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly turn on the optional family-sync feature (below). Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions

The app requests these Android permissions, used only for the stated purpose:

  • Notifications / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED / WAKE_LOCK — to show your drink reminders on time.
  • INTERNET — used only when you enable optional family sync, so the app can share a leaderboard with members of your family.
  • The app does not access contacts, SMS, location, camera, or storage.

Optional family-sync feature (Beta)

Family sync lets you share a leaderboard with people on other devices. To use it, you connect the app to a relay server URL that you provide. WaterMinder does not operate any server, and it does not send your data to any third-party service.

  • Only the current day's totals and your chosen member name are transmitted to your relay server.
  • The relay server operator (you or your family) controls how that data is stored.
  • Disabling family sync stops all transmission.

Data sharing

WaterMinder does not sell or share your data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not employ analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children's privacy

Family profiles are stored locally and are controlled by the device owner. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

Your choices

  • Change or delete the daily goal and reminders at any time in Settings.
  • Delete local family profiles from the Leaderboard screen.
  • Remove all app data by uninstalling the app.
